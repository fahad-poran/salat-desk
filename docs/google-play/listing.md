**Full description** (4,000 characters max)

```
Turn an old Android phone into an always-on prayer clock for your desk.

Salat Desk shows today's prayer times, your mosque's Jamaat times and a live countdown. Between prayers it switches to a dim, slowly moving clock with a new Quran ayah or hadith every minute — designed to stay on all day without burning in the screen.

PRAYER TIMES
• Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha calculated on the phone, offline
• Calculation methods: Karachi, Muslim World League, ISNA, Egyptian, Dubai, Kuwait, Singapore, Moonsighting Committee or your own angles
• Hanafi or Standard Asr, and minute adjustments for each prayer

YOUR MOSQUE'S JAMAAT
• Enter your mosque's Jamaat timetable once; it can change from a chosen date
• Live countdown to the next Waqt or Jamaat
• Warning before each Jamaat at a time you choose for each prayer, with a blinking red frame
• Optional notification with vibration and the notification light, even when the app is in the background

ALWAYS-ON DISPLAY
• Dim grey clock on pure black that moves every minute, so no pixel stays lit
• Full dashboard returns automatically before each Jamaat
• Smaller, dimmer night clock from 23:00 until Fajr
• One-tap AOD button

QURAN AND HADITH
• A new ayah or hadith every minute, in Arabic with English translation and the exact reference
• Only texts that fit the screen completely are shown — never half a verse
• About 70 new ayat and 200 new hadith downloaded each week over Wi-Fi
• Library of everything shown, most recent first; remove anything you don't want to see

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no analytics
• Your settings stay on your phone
• Open source: github.com/fahad-poran/salat-desk

Calculated prayer times are astronomical estimates. Always follow your local mosque.

Quran text: Tanzil.net (Uthmani) and Sahih International, via AlQuran.cloud. Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, An-Nawawi's Forty Hadith and Forty Hadith Qudsi, via the open hadith-api.
```
