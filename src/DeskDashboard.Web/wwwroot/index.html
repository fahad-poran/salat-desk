Configuration

Settings

Location

Where you pray. Prayer times are calculated for this place. Search for a city, or enter the coordinates and an IANA time zone such as Asia/Dhaka.

Prayer times

How the Waqt (start) times are calculated. Choose the method your mosque follows.

Advanced: manual adjustments

Shift each calculated time by a few minutes to match your mosque's printed timetable. 0 keeps the calculation.

Jamaat times

Choose for each prayer how its Jamaat time is set: a fixed time, a number of minutes after the Waqt, or the next rounded clock time after the Waqt. The preview shows what you get on the chosen date.

Jamaat times need review

These fixed times are set to "keep the time and warn" and no longer fit their prayer window. Change the time, or choose what happens when the Waqt passes it.

    Jamaat alerts

    Before each Jamaat the screen shows a red, blinking warning with the time left, and the phone can show a notification, even when Salat Desk is closed. Choose when the warning starts for each prayer (it never starts before the Waqt).

    Prayer Notify Minutes before Jamaat

    Notifications are created on this phone only, for the next 7 days, and are planned again every day and after a restart. The notification light usually blinks only while the screen is off.

    When blinking is off, the warning stays red but still.

    Display & always-on

    Between prayers the screen switches to a dim clock that moves every minute, which protects it from burn-in. The full dashboard returns before each Jamaat and for two minutes after you tap the screen.

    Brightness applies only while this app is open; the phone's own brightness setting is not changed.

    Appearance

    The clock font and the accent colour used for highlights. Changes show straight away.

    Quran & Hadith

    In the always-on view a new ayah or hadith appears every minute, only when it fits the screen completely.

    Loading content status…

    About 70 new Ayat and 200 new Hadith are downloaded once a week over Wi-Fi. Use the Quran and Hadith buttons on the dashboard to read everything, copy a reference, or remove anything you don't want shown.

    Quran Arabic: Tanzil.net (Uthmani). Quran English: Sahih International. Both via AlQuran.cloud. Hadith Arabic and English: fawazahmed0/hadith-api, from Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, An-Nawawi's Forty Hadith and Forty Hadith Qudsi. Everything downloaded is stored on this device and works offline.
